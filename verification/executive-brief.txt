EXECUTIVE SUMMARY · VISION IN DEVELOPMENT

Sanctuary LV is developing a new kind of gathering space for Las Vegas at the intersection of faith,
creativity, culture, community, hospitality, and live experiences.
The vision goes beyond a traditional Sunday-use church facility: a flexible environment that serves people, supports creative
expression, and brings the community together throughout the week.
