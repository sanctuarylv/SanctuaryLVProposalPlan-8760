CURRENT FACT · VISION IN DEVELOPMENT

intersection of faith, creativity, culture, community, hospitality, and live experiences.
The vision goes beyond a traditional Sunday-use church facility: a flexible environment that serves people,
supports creative expression, and brings the community together throughout the week.
